Privacy Policy

App: Animal Jigsaw • Studio: LIA Studio • Last Updated: August 2026

Animal Jigsaw is designed specifically for children aged 3–7. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). The game is 100% ad-free and equipped with a verified Parental Gate for in-app purchases.

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

LIA (Luz Interactive Application) / LIA Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of young children and families. This Privacy Policy explains our data handling practices for Animal Jigsaw.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection Policy

We strictly adhere to a zero-collection policy for minors:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, birthdates, or physical addresses.
  • No Geolocation Tracking: We do not access, track, or collect GPS or IP-based location data.
  • No Device Persistent Identifiers: We do not track Advertising IDs (GAID), IDFA, or unique hardware serial numbers for profiling.
  • No Audio or Camera Access: The application does not request camera, microphone, or photo library permissions.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising Policy

To provide an immersive, peaceful, and safe learning environment, Animal Jigsaw contains NO third-party advertisements (no banner ads, no full-screen interstitial ads, and no rewarded promotional videos).

4. In-App Purchases and Parental Gate

Animal Jigsaw offers optional, permanent non-consumable package unlocks. All store access is secured by a randomized 4-digit arithmetic gatekeeper to prevent unintended child transactions.

5. Global Child Privacy Compliance

Our practices fully comply with the following regulations:

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act - USA)
  • GDPR-K (General Data Protection Regulation for Kids - EU)
  • LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados - Brazil)
  • Google Play Families Policy Requirements
