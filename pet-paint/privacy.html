Privacy Policy

App: Pet Paint (com.lia.petpaint) • Studio: LIA Studio • Last Updated: November 2026

Pet Paint is designed specifically for young children aged 3–5. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit ANY Personally Identifiable Information (PII). The app is 100% Free, 100% Ad-Free, contains NO In-App Purchases, and requires ZERO device permissions (works completely offline).

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

LIA (Luz Interactive Application) / LIA Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and safety of young children and families. This Privacy Policy explains our zero-data practices for Pet Paint (com.lia.petpaint).

2. Zero Personal Data Collection Policy

We strictly adhere to a zero-collection policy for children and all users:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, birthdates, or physical addresses.
  • No Geolocation or Network Tracking: Pet Paint operates 100% offline and does not access GPS, IP addresses, or external servers.
  • No Advertising or Analytics SDKs: We do not include any third-party analytics, crash-reporting trackers, Advertising IDs (GAID), or profiling SDKs.
  • Zero Device Permissions: The application does not request camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or internet permissions.

3. 100% Free, Zero Ads, Zero In-App Purchases

Pet Paint contains NO advertisements (no banners, no interstitials, no rewarded videos) and NO in-app purchases. All 40 coloring templates and playground interactions are free for every child.

4. Local-Only Storage & Parental Gate

Children's colored artwork and playground creations are saved strictly in private local storage on the user's own device and are never uploaded anywhere. Resetting local saved artwork is protected behind a Parents Only Gate to prevent accidental deletion by young children.

5. Global Child Privacy Compliance

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act - USA)
  • GDPR-K (General Data Protection Regulation for Kids - EU)
  • LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados - Brazil)
  • Google Play Families Policy & Teacher Approved Standards
