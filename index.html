💬 06. Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Parents Ask Us

Common inquiries about our development policies, privacy, and future roadmap

Q1 Are all LIA Studio apps completely ad-free?

Yes, 100%! All titles developed by LIA Studio strictly adhere to a zero-ad policy. We never display third-party popups, banners, or video commercials.

Q2 How is my child's privacy protected?

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Our apps function securely on your device in compliance with global child safety standards.

Q3 Can we play games offline while traveling?

Absolutely. Once downloaded, all stages and reward celebration videos are fully accessible offline without requiring an active Wi-Fi or cellular connection.

Q4 What upcoming projects is LIA working on?

Beyond toddler puzzles, we are actively developing interactive mathematics and cognitive logic tools designed from early childhood to teenage learners.
